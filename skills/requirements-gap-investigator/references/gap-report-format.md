# Requirements gap report format

Use this template for a request with several stakeholders, meaningful ambiguity, or a conflict worth surfacing. Omit sections that add no value, but preserve the stated/reported/inferred/unknown distinction.

## Request as stated

- **Requester(s):**
- **Request, in their own words:**
- **Stated goal, if any:**
- **Deadline or dependency, if any:**

## Investigation status

Choose one:

- **Clear enough to draft from:** the gaps found are minor and don't block writing requirements.
- **Gaps identified:** one or more real gaps exist; requirements should not be drafted until they're resolved.
- **Too ambiguous to proceed:** the request cannot be usefully scoped without stakeholder input first.

## Gaps and conflicts

| Classification | Finding | Source | What it blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stated, reported, inferred, or unknown | Concise finding | Message, ticket, doc, or "not available" | What can't be safely assumed until this is resolved |

## Competing interpretations

Where genuinely ambiguous, list each interpretation and what would confirm or rule it out.

## Recommended confirmations — not performed

List what to confirm and with whom before requirements are drafted. Make clear nothing has been drafted yet.
